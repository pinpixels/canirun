What it does

GPU fit badges

Adds a colored badge next to each quant or file on a Hugging Face or Ollama library model page showing whether it fits your GPU's VRAM (green fits comfortably, yellow tight, red won't fit).

Estimated tokens/sec

Shows a rough tokens-per-second estimate for each quant based on your selected GPU class and the model's parameter count.

One-click ollama pull tag

Shows the exact `ollama pull :` command for the quant you're viewing, with a button to copy it to the clipboard.

GPU and VRAM presets

Pick your GPU from a list of common consumer and prosumer GPUs, or enter a custom VRAM/RAM amount. The choice is saved on your device for future visits.

On/off toggle

A checkbox in the popup turns fit badges on or off, so you can quickly compare the page with and without them.

Privacy

VRAM Fit Check keeps everything on your device. It contains no code that sends data anywhere, and has no account, ads or analytics.
