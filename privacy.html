VRAM Fit Check Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27 · Version 1.0.0

VRAM Fit Check is a browser extension published by PinPixels ("we", "us"). Contact: pinpixels@gmail.com · Issues.

In short

  • VRAM Fit Check works entirely on your device. It contains no code that sends data to us or anyone else.
  • We have no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or anything unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

What VRAM Fit Check handles

VRAM Fit Check does not collect or store personal data.

All data stays in your browser's extension storage on your computer. We cannot read, recover or delete it for you. You can delete it any time by removing items in the extension or uninstalling it.

Permissions

  • storage: Stores the user's settings and data locally on their device with chrome.storage. Nothing is transmitted.
  • clipboardWrite: Copies text to the clipboard when the user presses a copy button.
  • https://huggingface.co/* (site access): Needed to read the model and quant names already shown on the page, so a fit badge can be added next to them. Runs only on these two sites, reads no other data, and sends nothing to any server.
  • https://ollama.com/* (site access): Needed to read the model and quant names already shown on the page, so a fit badge can be added next to them. Runs only on these two sites, reads no other data, and sends nothing to any server.

Limited Use and third parties

VRAM Fit Check's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No data is transferred to any third party.

Your choices and rights

You control your data on your device. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU/UK or California you have the usual rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; email pinpixels@gmail.com.

Children

VRAM Fit Check is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and material changes are listed in the changelog.
